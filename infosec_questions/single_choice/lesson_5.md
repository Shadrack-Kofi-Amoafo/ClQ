# Information Security MCQs — Lesson 5 (Single Choice)

Source: `lesson 5.pdf` (Introduction to Information Security Strategy and Policy Management)

**Format:** Choose the single best answer (A-E).

**Mix:** Q1-Q75 are application/understanding-focused; Q76-Q100 are core knowledge checks.

---
## Q1. An employee claims they didn't know the company password policy because it was never shared with staff. Which enforcement criterion failed?

- A) Dissemination
- B) Review
- C) Comprehension
- D) Compliance
- E) Uniform enforcement

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Dissemination means distributing the policy to affected individuals.

---

## Q2. Employees received the policy but most never read it. Which criterion failed?

- A) Dissemination
- B) Review
- C) Comprehension
- D) Compliance
- E) Uniform enforcement

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Review means the policy is read.
