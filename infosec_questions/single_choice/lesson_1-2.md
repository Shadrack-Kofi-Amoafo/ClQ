# Information Security MCQs — Lesson 1 & 2 (Single Choice)

Source: `lesson 1-2.pdf` (INF 413 – Information Systems Security and Management)

**Format:** Choose the single best answer (A-E).

**Mix:** Q1-Q75 are application/understanding-focused; Q76-Q100 are core knowledge checks.

---

## Q1. A company installs CCTV cameras after a laptop theft to identify future culprits. Which security principle is most directly being applied?

- A) Prevention
- B) Detection
- C) Recovery
- D) Non-repudiation
- E) Authorization

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** CCTV supports detection by helping identify when, how, and by whom an asset was compromised or removed.

---

## Q2. After a ransomware incident, an organization restores systems from clean backups and returns services to normal. This is best described as:

- A) Prevention
- B) Detection
- C) Reaction
- D) Authentication
- E) Accountability

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Reaction includes responding to an incident and recovering assets or recovering from damage.

---

## Q3. An administrator enforces account lockout and strong password rules to stop brute-force attempts before they succeed. This is primarily:

- A) Prevention
- B) Detection
- C) Reaction
- D) Auditing
- E) Non-repudiation

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Prevention takes measures to stop assets from being damaged or compromised.
