## Q1. A company deploys a new policy but employees never receive it. What should be done?

- A) Disseminate/distribute the policy
- B) Obtain acknowledgment/review
- C) Train employees
- D) Assume everyone knows it
- E) Keep policy hidden

**Answer:** A, B, C

**Explanation:** Dissemination, review, and training support policy effectiveness.

---

## Q2. Employees sign policy but don't understand technical terms. What is needed?

- A) Improve comprehension through plain language/training
- B) Assess understanding
- C) Provide examples/guidance
- D) Ignore comprehension
- E) Punish without clarification

**Answer:** A, B, C

**Explanation:** Comprehension requires understanding; training and clarity help.
